Deviation Hunt — notes for the Twitch Extensions reviewer (paste into "Review Notes / Testing Instructions" when submitting)

WHAT IT IS
Deviation Hunt is a chat game run by our chat bot (OHDeviationHunt). While a stream is live, the bot posts that a deviation was spotted; viewers type !secure to try to catch it. This panel shows the viewer's own collection ("Securement Pods"), lets them scrap duplicate specimens, and has a Shop where they spend Starchrom (an in-game currency earned only by playing) on Securement Units, Capture Soup and gloves. No real money and no Bits in this version (Bits support is off).

HOW TO TEST
1. Install the panel on any channel and open it. Click "Show my Securement Pods" to share your Twitch identity (required; without it the panel only shows that button).
2. A viewer who has never played sees every deviation as "???" (not caught yet), and the Shop tab explains how to start. The Commands tab works for everyone.
3. To get data: the game is live on twitch.tv/imbon3s during streams. Type !secure in chat when the bot announces a deviation; after the result is posted, the panel shows your Securement Pods, and the Shop is usable (new players start with 200 Starchrom and 5 Securement Units).
   To test on your own channel instead: add the bot at https://deviationhunt.ohwikiguide.com ("Add to my channel"), go live, and type !hunt spawn as the broadcaster to spawn a deviation right away.
4. If you need test data on your account without going live, email ohtwitchbot@ohwikiguide.com with your Twitch username and we'll add some.

EXTERNAL CONNECTIONS
- API: https://deviationhunt.ohwikiguide.com (our server; requests carry the Twitch extension JWT, verified with the extension secret).
- Images: https://ohwikiguide.com (deviation pictures from our wiki) and https://deviationhunt.ohwikiguide.com (shop item pictures).
- The only link that leaves the panel is "Open my collection page", which opens the viewer's public collection page on deviationhunt.ohwikiguide.com in a new tab.
